# T_MTS_PortFrame: image prompt

Prompt to recreate the background of the port button with an image generator. The current file is generated by
`Tools/generate_ui_icons.py` (`port_frame`); use this prompt only for a hand-made or AI-made variant, then check it
against `../Preview_PortButton_States.png`.

## Prompt

Flat 2D game UI icon of a conveyor belt port, seen straight from the front, square 256 x 256 pixels, transparent
background. A thick rounded square frame fills almost the whole image (about 12 pixels of margin), with corner
radius about 28 pixels, painted dark gunmetal grey (#3A3D42) with a lighter steel grey outline (#6B7078) about
8 pixels wide. In each of the four corners sits a small round bolt head, light grey (#8A9099), about 14 pixels
across. In the upper middle of the frame is a recessed opening: a rounded rectangle about 160 pixels wide and
156 pixels high, corner radius about 16 pixels, filled with near-black (#15171A) and edged by a thin steel grey
line (#6B7078). Below the opening, along the bottom of the frame, runs a narrow horizontal hazard stripe band about
176 pixels wide and 22 pixels high, with diagonal stripes leaning to the right, alternating safety orange (#FA9549)
and dark charcoal (#282A2E). Clean industrial sci-fi style matching the Satisfactory game interface, crisp edges,
flat colors with at most a very subtle shading, centered and symmetric.

## Avoid

Perspective or 3D angle, drop shadows outside the frame, background color, text, logos, arrows or symbols inside
the opening, glow, noise, photorealistic textures, extra bolts or panels.

## Notes

- The opening must stay empty: the arrows are a separate layer (`T_MTS_PortArrows`, a 2 x 2 atlas) that the widget
  clips to the inside of the opening and slides to change state. Keep the opening at the same place and size, or
  update `OPENING_RECT` in the generator and the widget's clip area to match.
- Import settings as for the other UI textures: Texture Group UI, Compression UserInterface2D, Mip Gen NoMipmaps.
